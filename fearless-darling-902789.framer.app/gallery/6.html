Drone-Based Forest Monitoring & Surveillance


  • Conducting aerial surveys for rapid assessment of forest landscapes and natural resources.

  • Monitoring plantation growth, forest health, and restoration activities from a bird's-eye view.

  • Supporting wildlife habitat assessment and monitoring of ecologically sensitive areas.

  • Assisting in the detection of forest fires, illegal activities, and encroachments in remote locations.

  • Generating high-resolution imagery and spatial data for planning and management purposes.

  • Improving efficiency of field inspections by covering large areas in significantly less time.

  • Supporting GIS-based mapping, geo-spatial analysis, and digital forest management initiatives.

  • Enabling accurate documentation and monitoring of conservation and development projects.

  • Enhancing transparency, accountability, and evidence-based decision-making through aerial data collection.

  • Strengthening the division's commitment to adopting innovative technologies for sustainable forest governance.

Key Applications

🚁 Plantation Monitoring
🚁 Forest Fire Assessment
🚁 Wildlife Habitat Mapping
🚁 Encroachment Detection
🚁 Eco-Tourism Site Documentation
🚁 GIS & Spatial Analysis
🚁 Forest Infrastructure Monitoring
🚁 Landscape Change Detection
