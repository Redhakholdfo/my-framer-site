Redhakhol Forest Division

“ଜଙ୍ଗଲ ସୁରକ୍ଷା, ଜୀବନ ରକ୍ଷା।”

“ଜଙ୍ଗଲ ସୁରକ୍ଷା, ଜୀବନ ରକ୍ଷା।”

The Redhakhol Forest Division in Odisha's Sambalpur district spans about 1870.67 sq. km and hosts rich wildlife, including elephants, sloth bears, leopards, deer, wild boars, and various birds and reptiles. It covers dense forests like Hatidhara, Landakota, and Suhani, forming part of a vital elephant corridor. The forest department, with support from Village Protection Committees, works on wildlife monitoring, rescue, and anti-poaching. The division also tackles forest fires and human-wildlife conflicts through coordinated efforts and awareness drives, playing a key role in the state’s conservation efforts.
